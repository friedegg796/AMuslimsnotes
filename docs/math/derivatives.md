# Derivatives

A Derivative is the slope at a specific point of a graph.

The slope most people are familiar with ( aka the Change in Y/Change in X or better known as Y2-Y1/X2-X1 ) is an average slope of two points, not of a specific point.


## Finding a Derivative

In order to find one, you use a slope of a small difference, and then shrink it towards 0

## Simple Dervative Trick (The Drop and Subtract Method)

Works only for power functions

1: Take a number like x^2

2: put the exponent (the 2) in the front, making 2x^2

3: Subtract the remainng exponent by one, making 2x

The derivative for x^2 is 2x
