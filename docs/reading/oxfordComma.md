# Oxford Commas

Oxford commas are a comma before a conjunction (words like and) in lists of 3+ items.

EX: "I like A, B, and C."

The oxford comma here is the "," comes after "B"

These types of commas are completely optional, so a sentence of 

"I like A, B and C."

is fine.
